# Supplier Risk Management Policy

**Policy ID:** SRM-2023-03  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 2023-04-01  
**Owner:** VP of Supply Chain

## 1. Scope
This policy governs the onboarding, ongoing monitoring, and risk mitigation for all third-party suppliers, vendors, and service providers to RiskSentinel.

## 2. Supplier Criticality Rating
All suppliers must be categorized during onboarding based on their impact on business operations.

- **Critical:** Single source of essential materials/services. Disruption causes immediate production halt or service failure.
- **High:** Important materials/services. Disruption causes significant delay but alternative sources exist with effort.
- **Medium:** Standard commodities or services. Easily replaceable.
- **Low:** Non-essential services. Disruption has negligible impact.

## 3. Risk Monitoring Thresholds

### 3.1 Delivery Performance
- **Warning Threshold:** Average delivery delay exceeds 3 days over a 30-day period.
- **Breach Threshold:** SLA breached on >10% of orders or any delay exceeding 14 days.
- **Action:** Issue formal cure notice. Require root cause analysis from supplier.

### 3.2 Financial Health
- **Warning Threshold:** Significant drop in commercial credit rating or negative news regarding liquidity.
- **Action:** Finance team to conduct urgent review. Pause future long-term commitments.

### 3.3 Quality and Compliance
- **Warning Threshold:** Quality acceptance rate drops below 95%.
- **Breach Threshold:** Any compliance failure (e.g., labor laws, environmental regulations).
- **Action:** Immediate suspension of new orders pending audit.

## 4. Escalation and Mitigation Strategies

### Level 1: Standard Review (Low/Medium Criticality)
- Handled by Procurement Manager. Quarterly performance reviews.

### Level 2: Enhanced Monitoring (High Criticality or Level 1 Breach)
- Handled by Category Director. Monthly reviews. 
- **Mitigation:** Identify and qualify a secondary supplier. Increase buffer inventory by 20%.

### Level 3: Crisis Management (Critical Supplier Failure)
- Handled by VP of Supply Chain.
- **Mitigation:** Immediate activation of backup suppliers regardless of cost premium. Reallocation of existing inventory. Daily briefings to Executive Team.

## 5. Termination
A supplier contract may be terminated for cause if:
1. They fail to remediate a breach threshold within 30 days of a cure notice.
2. They are involved in severe ethical or legal violations.
3. They experience a cyber breach that materially impacts RiskSentinel data.
