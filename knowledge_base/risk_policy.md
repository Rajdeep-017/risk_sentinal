# Enterprise Risk Management Policy

**Policy ID:** ERM-2023-01  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15  
**Owner:** Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Policy establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and reporting risks across RiskSentinel and all its subsidiaries. The scope encompasses all operational, financial, cyber, customer, and strategic risks.

## 2. Risk Appetite Statement
RiskSentinel operates within a moderate risk appetite overall, balancing aggressive growth targets with prudent controls. 
- **Financial Risk:** Low appetite for liquidity risk.
- **Cyber Risk:** Zero tolerance for systemic data breaches.
- **Compliance:** Zero tolerance for regulatory violations.
- **Operational:** Moderate tolerance for temporary service degradations (up to 4 hours).

## 3. Risk Thresholds and Categorization
Risks are evaluated on a 1-100 scale, combining Probability (1-10) and Impact (1-10).

### 3.1 Low Risk (Score: 1-20)
- **Definition:** Routine operational anomalies. Minimal financial or reputational impact.
- **Required Action:** Logging and standard monthly review.
- **Approval Authority:** Line Manager.

### 3.2 Moderate Risk (Score: 21-50)
- **Definition:** Noticeable performance degradation or localized financial impact (<$50,000).
- **Required Action:** Active monitoring, root cause analysis within 7 days.
- **Approval Authority:** Department Head.

### 3.3 High Risk (Score: 51-75)
- **Definition:** Significant financial impact ($50,000 - $500,000), material service disruption (4-12 hours), or emerging regulatory concerns.
- **Required Action:** Immediate mitigation plan development. Weekly reporting to Risk Committee.
- **Approval Authority:** VP / SVP.

### 3.4 Critical Risk (Score: 76-100)
- **Definition:** Existential threat to the organization. Impact >$500,000, severe reputational damage, or systemic failure.
- **Required Action:** Immediate execution of business continuity or crisis response plan. Daily updates to Executive Board.
- **Approval Authority:** C-Suite / Board of Directors.

## 4. Reporting Requirements
- **Daily:** Automated dashboards for key risk indicators (KRIs).
- **Weekly:** Department-level risk reviews for High/Critical items.
- **Monthly:** Comprehensive risk report to Executive Team.
- **Quarterly:** Formal presentation to the Board Risk Committee.

## 5. Exceptions
Any override of automated risk controls must be documented in the exception log, citing business justification, and approved by a VP or higher. Exceptions are valid for a maximum of 90 days.
