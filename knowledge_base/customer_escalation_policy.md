# Customer Escalation and Retention Policy

**Policy ID:** CER-2023-02  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Effective Date:** 2023-02-15  
**Owner:** VP of Customer Success

## 1. Scope
This policy outlines the procedures for identifying at-risk customers, managing severe complaints, and executing retention strategies. It applies to all B2B and high-value B2C accounts.

## 2. Risk Indicators and Thresholds
A customer is considered "At-Risk" if any of the following triggers are met:

### 2.1 Usage Decline
- A drop in platform utilization of >20% over a 30-day period compared to their historical average.
- **Action:** Automated email check-in and task assigned to Account Manager.

### 2.2 Satisfaction and Complaints
- CSAT score drops below 3.0 (out of 5).
- More than 3 support tickets opened in a 7-day period.
- **Action:** Mandatory phone call from Customer Success Manager (CSM) within 48 hours.

### 2.3 Financial Distress
- Payment delayed by >15 days past due date.
- **Action:** Finance team initiates soft collection; CSM attempts contact to renegotiate terms if necessary.

## 3. Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Standard Issue
- **Criteria:** General technical issue or billing inquiry.
- **Resolution Target:** 24 hours.
- **Owner:** Tier 1 Support.

### Level 2: High Priority
- **Criteria:** Core functionality impaired; at-risk indicators triggered.
- **Resolution Target:** 8 hours.
- **Owner:** Tier 2 Support / Dedicated CSM.

### Level 3: Critical Escalation
- **Criteria:** Complete service outage for the client; explicit threat to churn; executive complaint.
- **Resolution Target:** 2 hours.
- **Owner:** Director of Customer Success.

## 4. Retention Authority and Concessions
To prevent churn, authorized staff may offer concessions based on the Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

- **CSM:** Authorized to offer up to 10% discount for 3 months or $500 in service credits.
- **Director:** Authorized to offer up to 25% discount for 6 months or $2,500 in service credits.
- **VP:** Required for any concession exceeding $2,500 or contract renegotiations.

## 5. Post-Mortem Requirement
For any churn event of a Top 100 Account (by MRR), a formal churn post-mortem must be conducted within 14 days and presented to the Executive Team, detailing root causes and systemic fixes required.
