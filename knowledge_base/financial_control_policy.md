# Financial Control and Liquidity Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-2023-01  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-01  
**Owner:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

## 1. Scope
This policy establishes the minimum acceptable standards for liquidity management, credit control, and margin maintenance to ensure the financial solvency of RiskSentinel.

## 2. Key Financial Risk Indicators (KRIs)

### 2.1 Operating Margin
- **Target:** > 15%
- **Warning Threshold:** < 10% for two consecutive months.
- **Critical Threshold:** < 5% in any single month.
- **Action on Warning:** Implement immediate cost-containment measures on discretionary spending.

### 2.2 Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- **Target:** < 45 days
- **Warning Threshold:** > 60 days.
- **Critical Threshold:** > 75 days.
- **Action on Warning:** Escalate collections process. Pause new credit extensions to accounts >60 days past due.

### 2.3 Credit Utilization (Revolving Facility)
- **Target:** < 30% utilization.
- **Warning Threshold:** > 50% utilization.
- **Critical Threshold:** > 75% utilization.
- **Action on Warning:** CFO must approve any further drawdowns. Review short-term cash flow forecast.

### 2.4 Payment Default Rate
- **Target:** < 2% of total receivables.
- **Warning Threshold:** > 4%.
- **Action on Warning:** Review underwriting standards. Increase bad debt reserve allowance.

## 3. Escalation Protocols

### Level 1: Departmental Action
- Triggered by any metric hitting a "Warning Threshold."
- Finance Director implements standard remedial actions (e.g., increased collection calls, tightening credit terms).

### Level 2: Executive Intervention
- Triggered if two or more KRIs hit "Warning Thresholds" simultaneously, or any single KRI hits a "Critical Threshold."
- CFO initiates Cash Conservation Mode. Hiring freezes and travel bans automatically enacted.

### Level 3: Board Notification
- Triggered if liquidity projections indicate inability to meet obligations within 60 days, or risk of covenant breach on credit facilities.
- CFO and CEO must immediately notify the Board of Directors. Emergency capital raise or asset sale contingency plans activated.

## 4. Approval Authorities for Exceptional Spending
During Level 2 (Cash Conservation Mode):
- All capital expenditures (CapEx) >$10,000 require CFO approval.
- All new vendor contracts require CFO approval.
- Pre-payment of expenses is prohibited.

## 5. Audit and Compliance
The internal audit team will review compliance with these thresholds quarterly. Any unauthorized deviations will be reported directly to the Audit Committee of the Board.
